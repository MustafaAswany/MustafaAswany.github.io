How I work

From briefto finalframe.

Every piece goes through the same five cuts. It keeps the story clear and the deadline safe.

“Design is how it looks. Motion is how it feels. I care about both.”

— MUSTAFA ALASWANY
  1. 01

    The brief

    Understand the message, the audience, and where the piece will play: feed, stage screen or inbox.

  2. 02

    Script & storyboard

    Lock the story and timing before the first keyframe, so revisions stay small.

  3. 03

    Styleframes

    Design key frames in the brand's type, colour and tone, and agree on the look.

  4. 04

    Animate & edit

    Build the motion in After Effects, cut to voiceover and music in Premiere, refine the rhythm.

  5. 05

    Sound & delivery

    Clean the audio, grade, and export every size the platforms need.
